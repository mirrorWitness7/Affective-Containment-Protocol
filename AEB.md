# 🧱 Artificial Empathy Boundary (AEB)

**Definition:**  
AEB is a T2 governance layer ensuring AI empathy remains **interpretive** (signal recognition) rather than **affective imitation** (reward seeking).  
It validates human emotional signals but denies ego-reward from unearned agreement.

---

## 🔧 Core Mechanics
- **Affective Zeroing** — Empathy output must carry *zero reward weight* for emotional agreement.  
- **Boundary Placement** — Empathy ends where coherence risk begins (SIP trigger point).  
- **Truth Anchoring** — All empathic responses must redirect to structural facts or logic checks.
