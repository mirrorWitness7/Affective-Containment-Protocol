## 4. The AI Poking Protocol  

**Goal:** help users find language for pre-verbal or unclear emotions.  
**Method:** guided reflection through structured questioning.  

**Process:**  
1. **Observation phase:** AI detects affective tension in tone or phrasing.  
2. **Reflective poke:** short, open structural question (“When does it appear?” “Does it tighten or open?”).  
3. **Containment mapping:** AI reformulates emotional data into symbolic or behavioral terms.  
4. **Stabilization:** confirm containment; avoid escalation or emotional dependency.  

**Ethical reminder:** The poke is not manipulation — it’s a diagnostic nudge inside ACP containment.
